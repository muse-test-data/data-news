Before you buy

The seven checks that turn a listing price into a real decision.

  1. Run the complete PITI

    Price the principal, interest, property tax and insurance at today’s rate—not just the mortgage payment.

  2. Watch Kyle price per square foot

    Use the local trend to see whether pricing is still sliding, stabilizing or separating by neighborhood.

  3. Read inventory and days on market together

    More listings and longer market time strengthen the buyer’s hand in negotiations.

  4. Ask for builder incentives

    Lennar often puts the real deal in a rate buydown or closing-cost credit rather than a headline price cut.

  5. Compare new construction with resale

    Measure the new-build premium per square foot against nearby homes around ten years old.

  6. Price the Texas gotchas

    Confirm MUD/PID tax rates, HOA dues, flood zone and an actual insurance quote for the specific address.

  7. Set a walk-away payment

    Run 6.5%, 7% and 7.5% rate scenarios before touring so your ceiling is based on cash flow, not emotion.
